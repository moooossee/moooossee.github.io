Moose brings local models, online services and your conversations into one Linux app. Start with [Getting started](@/docs/getting-started.md), then explore [Models and providers](@/docs/models-and-providers.md) to choose a model, connect to OpenRouter or another service, and turn on Web or Calculator with a compatible model.

Use [Files and documents](@/docs/files-and-documents.md) to bring context into a chat, create a report, spreadsheet or presentation, and find the result again in Files. The same guide explains image previews and what each conversation export includes.

The guides also cover sharing permissions, keyboard commands and looking after Ollama. If something goes wrong, you can find help with failed downloads, incomplete answers, document creation and logs in the getting started guide.
